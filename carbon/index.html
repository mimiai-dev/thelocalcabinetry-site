Estimates of delivery emissions only, for 25 linear feet of cabinets. Your ZIP and kitchen size change the numbers.

Same cabinets, less room on the truck

Flat cartons stack tight. Built cabinets carry their empty insides with them, so they take up more of every truck.

How your kitchen gets to you
