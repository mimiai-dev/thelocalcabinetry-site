Our Policies

Shipping, returns, warranty, and how we handle your information.

Questions about any of this? Use our contact form and we'll get back to you.

Shipping & Returns

Processing & lead times

Lead time is about 7 days. Shipping within the US then takes 3 to 7 days, depending on your location. For custom work we confirm the production time when the order is placed.

Delivery

Cabinets ship flat-packed. Smaller items go by parcel carrier; full kitchens and larger orders ship by freight. Shipping is quoted with your order based on size and destination.

Inspect on delivery

  • Inspect all cartons when they arrive, before signing for the delivery.
  • If anything is damaged, note it on the delivery receipt and take photos.
  • Report shipping damage within 5 days of delivery and we'll send replacement parts at no charge. Please keep the original packaging.

Returns

If you're not satisfied, we'll gladly accept a return within 30 days of receiving your order. Submit a return request and we'll confirm by email within 1–4 business days; once we receive and inspect the items, we issue the refund to your original payment method.

  • Unassembled items in their original boxes: a 5–10% restocking fee applies.
  • Items assembled by us: a 20% restocking fee applies.
  • Return shipping is the customer's responsibility. If your order shipped with free shipping, the original outbound shipping cost is deducted from the refund.
  • Returns after 30 days are subject to approval based on elapsed time and condition.

Non-returnable

  • Custom-made or special-request items.
  • Items assembled by the customer, or modified (e.g. cut or added to).
  • Items removed from their original packaging.
  • Items missing pieces, or without the original invoice / sales order.

Damaged products

We want every item to arrive in perfect condition. If something arrives damaged, submit a replacement request within 10 days of delivery and we'll arrange the necessary replacements as quickly as possible at no extra cost. We may ask for photos to confirm the damage. If a full replacement unit is sent, the original item becomes our property, so please keep everything in its original packaging until we confirm next steps.

White-glove & freight deliveries

  • The person who placed the order should be present to inspect and sign for the delivery.
  • Inspect the product while the driver is on site and note any problems on the delivery document - even slight damage. If in doubt, write "Package Damaged" when you sign.
  • Issues not noted on the delivery document at the time of delivery may limit the solutions we can offer.

Cancellations

Because we process orders quickly, there's a limited window to cancel. Email us at info@thelocalcabinetry.com with the subject "Cancellation"; we'll confirm in writing within 2 business days. An order can't be cancelled once it has shipped, delivered, or been assembled for delivery. Approved cancellations are refunded to the original payment method within 3–5 business days.

Warranty

We offer a 1-year limited warranty to the original purchaser, covering manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship under normal residential use. The warranty is non-transferable.

What's covered

Defects in materials or workmanship under normal use. We will repair or replace the affected part at our discretion.

What's not covered

  • Normal wear and tear.
  • Improper assembly, installation, or maintenance.
  • Misuse, accidents, or alterations.
  • Natural variation in wood grain and color, and changes over time from humidity or sunlight.

Making a claim

Contact us through the contact form with your order number and photos of the issue, and we'll take it from there.

Conditions of Use

  • Prices and quotes are confirmed at the time of order; orders are processed once payment is received.
  • Once an order enters processing it can't be changed - additional items are placed as a new order.
  • Merchandise remains the property of The Local Cabinetry until paid in full.
  • Tax-exempt (resale) orders require a valid resale certificate on file before the order is placed.
  • The Local Cabinetry is a brand of Luxlive Home LLC, based in Houston, Texas. These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Texas.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we collect the name, email, phone number, and message you provide.

How we use it

We use your details to respond to your enquiry and to quote and fulfill your order. We may also send you news and promotions by email, as described below. We do not sell your information.

News and promotions

If you are a customer or have contacted us, we may send you occasional emails about new products, news and promotions. You do not need to sign up for them, and you can stop them at any time.

  • Every news or promotion email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Your email app may also show its own Unsubscribe button, which works the same way.
  • When you unsubscribe, we stop promptly, and always within 10 business days.
  • If you have a design account, you can also turn these emails on or off under Email preferences in your account menu.
  • Unsubscribing does not stop emails about your own designs, quotes, orders and deliveries. Those are part of the work you asked us for.
  • If you live outside the United States, for example in Canada, the European Union or the United Kingdom, we send you news and promotions only if you have told us you want them.
  • This section applies from October 29, 2026. If you contacted us before then, we send you news and promotions only if you tell us you want them.

How it's stored

Your contact details are stored securely and encrypted. You can ask us to access or delete your information at any time via the contact form.

Visits to this site

We keep a simple log on our own server of how the site is used: which pages are opened, which links and buttons are clicked, how long a page is actively open, and the website a visit came from. It holds no names and no form entries. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, we log nothing and set no cookie.

When you send a form, start a chat or sign in to our design page, we also note how you found us: the first page you opened in that browser tab, the name of the website you came from, and the campaign labels in a link (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign). We keep this only to see which sources bring enquiries. It is held for the tab only and is never stored in a cookie.

Cookie

We set one first-party cookie, called tlc_v. It is a random code with no personal data, it lasts 12 months, and we use it only to count returning visitors.

Chat

Messages you send in the chat go to our team. If a message cannot be delivered, the first 80 characters of it are kept for 14 days so that we can recover it. Text you typed but did not send is not stored; we only count how many characters it had.

Your email in the chat

To start a chat we ask for your email address. We use it for your project: to send you our reply and your design, and to follow up on your enquiry. We may also send news and promotions to it, as described under News and promotions. It is kept with your customer file, and in this browser so you are not asked again; it is sent to us only when you send your first message. If we reply while you are away and you have not seen it after about 10 minutes, we send one email with our reply and a link back to the chat, at most one a day. You can ask us to delete it at any time.
