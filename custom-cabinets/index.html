Custom & ready to assemble

Custom cabinets, made to size and shipped ready to assemble

The Local Cabinetry does both: ready-to-assemble (RTA) cabinets from our standard range, and custom work built to your measurements. Kitchens, bathroom vanities, closets, pantries and built-ins, designed with you and delivered anywhere in the US.

Design your cabinets · get a free design & quote

What we build to your size

  • Flat-door cabinets at your exact size. With flat (slab) doors, the cabinet is built to the width, height and depth you need: a short wall, the space beside a window, a gap between appliances. A front wider than 48″ is split into two doors so it hangs straight.
  • Shaker and raised-panel runs planned to your walls. These doors come in set sizes, so we lay out the run from our standard cabinets and cut filler strips to close the gap to the wall.
  • A taller counter, with any door style. Standard base cabinets sit on a raised platform with levelers, so the counter comes out taller (for example 37-39″), shaker included.
  • Closets and wardrobes sized to the room. Closet fronts are flat, so towers, shelving, drawers and hanging rods are planned wall by wall. See closets & wardrobes.
  • Vanities sized to your bathroom. Single, double and floating vanity cabinets. See bathroom vanities.
  • Pantries, desks, benches and islands. Tall pantry cabinets, desk and bench cabinets, and islands planned from base cabinets, for kitchens, laundry rooms, offices and living spaces.
  • The finishing pieces. Glass-front doors, a panel-ready dishwasher front in your door finish, and matching crown molding in the same finish as the doors.

Door styles and finishes

Shaker, slim shaker, raised-panel and flat (slab) doors, in painted, woodgrain, matte and high-gloss finishes from our catalog: Shaker White, Navy Blue, Sage Breeze, Slim White Oak, Oak Blonde, Matte Black, High Glossy White and more. Browse them on door styles & finishes or in our catalog (PDF).

Ready to assemble, either way

Standard or custom, every cabinet ships flat-packed and goes together with standard tools. That keeps freight down and makes the boxes easy to carry in. In the Houston area we can also install for you; see Houston.

How it works

  1. Design. Start a layout in our design tool, or send your wall measurements and a few photos (our measuring guide walks you through it). We draw it in 3D for free.
  2. Quote. You get an itemized, no-obligation quote for the cabinets. The cabinet cost guide shows what a typical project runs. Countertops are by others.
  3. Build. We check every size with you on the drawing, then build. Lead time is about 7 days. Shipping within the US then takes 3 to 7 days, depending on your location. For custom work we confirm the production time when the order is placed.
  4. Ship. Your cabinets arrive flat-packed and labeled, ready to assemble.

Where we work

We take orders for delivery anywhere in the United States. The Local Cabinetry is based in Houston, Texas, with a showroom there that you can visit by appointment: contact us to set a day.

Custom cabinet FAQs

Do you make custom-size cabinets?

Yes. Cabinets with flat (slab) doors are built to the width, height and depth you need, for kitchens, vanities, closets and built-ins. Shaker, slim shaker and raised-panel doors come in set sizes, so those runs are planned from our standard cabinets with filler strips cut to close the gap to the wall. If you want a shaker look at a size that is not standard, we can suggest the closest flat-door option.

RTA or assembled?

Every cabinet ships ready to assemble (RTA): flat-packed, and it goes together with standard tools. Flat-packing keeps freight costs down and the boxes easy to carry into the house. In the Houston area we can also install for you; see our Houston page.

Which finishes and door styles can I choose?

Shaker, slim shaker, raised-panel and flat (slab) doors, in painted, woodgrain, matte and high-gloss finishes from our catalog, such as Shaker White, Navy Blue, Sage Breeze, Slim White Oak, Oak Blonde, Matte Black and High Glossy White. We help you pick the finish that suits the room.

Can you build a pantry, a desk, a bench or an island?

Yes. Besides base, wall and corner cabinets we build tall pantry cabinets, desk and bench cabinets, vanities and closet towers with hanging rods, and we plan islands from base cabinets. Glass-front doors, a panel-ready dishwasher front in your door finish and matching crown molding are available too.

How long do custom cabinets take?

Lead time is about 7 days. Shipping within the US then takes 3 to 7 days, depending on your location. For custom work we confirm the production time when the order is placed.

Can I return custom cabinets?

Custom-made and special-request items cannot be returned, so we check every size with you on the drawing before anything is built. Standard items follow our 30-day return policy.

Where do you deliver?

Anywhere in the United States. We are based in Houston, Texas, with a showroom there that you can visit by appointment.
