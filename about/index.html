About us

Cabinetry people, doing cabinetry properly.

The Local Cabinetry is a Houston, Texas cabinetry brand - the consumer storefront of Luxlive Home LLC - built on more than 18 years of custom-cabinetry manufacturing experience.

What we make

We make kitchen cabinets, bathroom vanities and closet systems, plus the cabinet doors, interior doors and hardware that go with them. Almost everything ships ready to assemble (RTA): flat-packed, precision-cut, hardware included, assembled with standard tools. That single decision - flat packs from stock instead of built boxes from a backlog - is why a made-to-your-measure kitchen can ship in days rather than months, and why it costs less to freight without arriving damaged.

How it's built

One build standard, whatever you order: ½″ cabinet-grade plywood boxes with UV-coated interiors - no particleboard - full-overlay doors, dovetailed solid-wood drawer boxes, and soft-close hinges and undermount glides throughout. Made in the USA, which also means zero import tariffs on your invoice.

The certifications, in plain language

  • TSCA Title VI. The U.S. federal formaldehyde-emission standard for composite wood. Every panel we ship complies - it is the law for wood products sold in the United States, and we certify to it.
  • KCMA. The Kitchen Cabinet Manufacturers Association testing program - cabinets are tested for structural integrity, door and drawer durability, and finish resistance to household spills and steam.
  • CARB2. California's Phase 2 formaldehyde limit, the strictest air-quality standard for composite wood in North America. Our boxes meet it nationwide, not just in California.
  • NGBS Green. The National Green Building Standard - third-party certification that the product line meets criteria for resource-efficient, lower-emission building products.
