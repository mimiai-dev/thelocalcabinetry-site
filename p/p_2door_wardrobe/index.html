Walk-in Closet - Oak

From $149.99 / linear ft - request a free quote for your exact layout.

A built-in walk-in closet system in warm oak, designed around open display: airy shelving for folded stacks and bags, single- and double-hang rails, and drawer banks where you want things behind a front. The light oak grain keeps a primary-bedroom closet feeling like furniture rather than fixture - it pairs naturally with brass or matte-black hardware and reads warm even under cool LED lighting.

The system is fully modular and configured to your room: tell us the wall lengths and ceiling height and we lay out the towers, shelves and rails around how you actually store. Carcasses are ½″ cabinet-grade plywood (no particleboard) with a UV-coated interior, shelving is adjustable, and drawer banks run on soft-close undermount glides with dovetailed solid-wood boxes.

Everything ships flat as a ready-to-assemble (RTA) system from our six U.S. warehouses and assembles with standard tools - a practical weekend project for a handy homeowner, or a fast install for your contractor. Closet systems are quoted per project from $149.99 per linear foot: an 8-foot closet wall starts around $1,200 in cabinetry, quoted exactly with a free 3D design. Custom dimensions and other finishes are available on request. TSCA Title VI, KCMA and CARB2 certified.
