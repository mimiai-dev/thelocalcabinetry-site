Modern Handleless Kitchen Cabinet

From $99.99 / linear ft - request a free quote for your exact layout.

A sleek European look: frameless, full-access cabinets with a flat slim front and an integrated grip rail in place of hardware. Because frameless construction has no face frame, you get wider drawers and a few extra inches of usable storage in the same footprint - and the unbroken door faces read as one continuous surface across the room.

Underneath, the build is the same standard as every cabinet we make: ½″ cabinet-grade plywood box with a UV-coated interior (no particleboard), dovetailed solid-wood drawer boxes, and soft-close hinges and undermount glides throughout. It ships flat as a ready-to-assemble (RTA) kitchen from our six U.S. warehouses and assembles with standard tools. Base cabinets run 34½″ high and 24″ deep; wall cabinets come in 30″, 36″ and 42″ heights, with tall, sink-base and corner units to complete the layout. Pair it with slab, slim-shaker or woodgrain fronts in finishes from matte white and sage to charcoal, walnut and light oak - free door samples available.

Quoted per project from $99.99 per linear foot (a typical 10×10 kitchen is about 25 linear feet - from roughly $2,500 in cabinets), with a free 3D design before you order. TSCA Title VI, KCMA and CARB2 certified.
