Walk-in Closet - Walnut

From $149.99 / linear ft - request a free quote for your exact layout.

A full dressing-room build in deep walnut woodgrain: wall-to-wall storage with double-hang rails for everyday clothes, long-hang sections for coats and dresses, angled shoe shelving, and banks of soft-close drawers for everything that shouldn't be on display. The dark, tailored grain gives the room a boutique feel - high contrast against light walls, and a natural match for glass-front sections and integrated LED strips.

Like all our closet systems it is modular and made to your space: send the room dimensions and we plan the elevation wall by wall, balancing hanging, shelving and drawer storage to your wardrobe. Carcasses are ½″ cabinet-grade plywood with a UV-coated interior - no particleboard - with adjustable shelving and dovetailed solid-wood drawer boxes on undermount glides.

The system ships flat, ready to assemble (RTA), from our six U.S. warehouses, and goes together with standard tools. Closet systems are quoted per project from $149.99 per linear foot - a 10-foot dressing-room wall starts around $1,500 in cabinetry, and your free 3D design prices the exact layout before you commit. Custom sizes and other finishes are available on request. TSCA Title VI, KCMA and CARB2 certified.
