## Rules for every brief
- One main keyword and one reader intent per post; answer the question in the first paragraph.
- Unique title (up to 70 characters) and description (70 to 170), a clear h2 structure, 2 or more internal links, our own images with alt text and size.
- Local angle only with facts we own (we ship from Houston and U.S. warehouses); no "made in" claims in news posts.
- Review quarterly: add what Search Console shows people actually search for, retire what does not work.
