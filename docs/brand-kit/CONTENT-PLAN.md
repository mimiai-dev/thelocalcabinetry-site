# Content pillars and the first 2 weeks

Matches the topics on our News page (thelocalcabinetry.com/news/). Posts below link to a guide when it is published; until then use the page named in brackets. No prices, no discounts, only approved claims (BRAND-GUIDE.md section 3). Every caption: one tip, one link.

## Pillars
1. **Measure and plan** (how to measure, island and closet planning): our strongest, most useful content.
2. **Choose your style** (door styles, finishes, collections): use the finish sheets.
3. **Know what you are buying** (RTA vs assembled, certifications, what is in the box): plain, honest explanations.
4. **Design with us** (free 3D layout and quote, what to prepare for a design call): the call to action.
5. **Membership** (10% off all orders, priority lead times, dedicated rep): one post a week at most.
