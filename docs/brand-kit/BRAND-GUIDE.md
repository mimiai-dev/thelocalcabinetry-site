# The Local Cabinetry: brand guide (v1, 2026-10-19)

For social media and marketing. Everything here is taken from our live site (thelocalcabinetry.com) and from rules the owner and lead have set. If something is not written here, ask before you post it.

## 1. Who we are
The Local Cabinetry is a Houston, Texas cabinetry brand (Luxlive Home LLC). We sell ready-to-assemble (RTA) kitchen cabinets, bathroom vanities and closets, designed with the customer (free 3D layout and quote) and shipped nationwide. Site: thelocalcabinetry.com. Email: info@thelocalcabinetry.com. Phone: (857) 930-8324.

## 2. Voice
Warm, plain, expert, short.
- Talk like a helpful person who knows cabinets, not like an ad. Short sentences. One idea per post.
- Say what the customer gets, not how great we are.
- Be exact with numbers and honest about limits (for example: we do not assemble or install, handymen and contractors can).
- English first. If a post is bilingual (English and Chinese), keep both short and say the same thing.
- No em dashes (the long dash). Use a comma, a colon or a new sentence.
- Light use of emoji is fine (0 to 2 per post). No hashtag walls: 3 to 5 relevant tags.

## 4. Do not say
- A blanket "Made in the USA" or "American-made" (true only for items our US workshop builds; do not use it unless the owner names the item).
- Any price, "from 99 dollars", sale, discount percentage or deal in a post unless the owner approved that exact post. Never quote trade, designer or distributor pricing (we have none).
- That members also get the AI-assistant offer, or any "stacking" of discounts. Only: "10% off all orders".
- "Zero import tariffs", "labeled boxes", "free shipping", "lifetime warranty", "same-day", "installed by us", or any delivery promise beyond the shipping line above.
- Words: cheap, luxury, bespoke, best, number one, guaranteed.
- Customer names, addresses, photos of homes or reviews unless we have written permission.

## 5. Visual style
- Clean, calm, lots of space. Navy and gold on white or warm cream. Serif headlines (Fraunces), plain sans text (Hanken Grotesk).
- One message per image. Large type. Our wordmark small in a corner, never stretched, never recolored (use the light or dark file as supplied).
- Logo clear space: at least the height of the letter "C" of the wordmark on every side. Minimum width: 120 px on screen.
- Avatar: use `tlc-avatar-navy-2000.png` (gold TLC on navy), safe for a circular crop.
- Photo style: bright, natural light, straight-on kitchens and closets, finishes shown in daylight. No heavy filters.

### Fonts
Fraunces (headlines, weights 400 to 600, italic for one emphasized word) and Hanken Grotesk (text, 400 to 700). Both are free (SIL Open Font License 1.1): files and licences are in `fonts/`. In Canva or Figma install the variable TTFs. If a tool cannot use them, use Georgia for headlines and Arial for text.

## 6. Images and video: only ours
Use only: our finish sheets and catalog pages (see ASSETS.md), the product photos listed there as cleared, and renders or photos we make ourselves. Do not use stock photos, photos from other brands, or any image that is not listed as cleared. If you are unsure where a picture came from, do not post it; ask.

## 7. Content preferences
- Teach first: how to measure, how to choose a door style, what a certification means. Sell second, with one clear next step: "Send us your room size, get a free 3D layout and quote."
- Every post ends with one action: the design page, the measuring guide, the catalog or the membership page.
- Link to the relevant page of our News section where one exists (thelocalcabinetry.com/news/), see CONTENT-PLAN.md.
- Reply to comments within a day; move anything about an order, a price or a problem to a private message or to info@thelocalcabinetry.com.
