Built for bathrooms, not just styled for them

A bathroom is the hardest room in the house on a cabinet: daily steam, splashed counters, wet hands on the same drawer front for years. That is why every one of our vanities uses the same ½″ cabinet-grade plywood box as our kitchens with a moisture-tolerant finish - plywood holds screws and keeps its shape in humidity where particleboard swells and fails. Drawer boxes are dovetailed solid wood on soft-close undermount glides, so the things you reach for every morning keep working like the day they were installed.

Single, double or floating

The standard footprint is 36″ wide × 21″ deep × 34½″ high, and the platform configures from a compact single-sink unit to a 72″ double-sink run - floor-standing in classic Shaker, or wall-mounted floating builds that keep the floor clear and the room feeling larger. Pick your width, finish, counter and hardware; add matching wall cabinets or a linen tower if the room allows. Free finish samples ship before you commit, and ready-to-assemble flat packs go together with standard tools.

How ordering works

Send the wall length and plumbing position, and we return a free 3D layout and an itemized quote priced per linear foot. Vanities ship flat from six U.S. warehouses, so most orders ship in days. Made in the USA; certified to TSCA Title VI, KCMA and CARB2 standards.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an RTA bathroom vanity cost?

Vanity runs start at $119.99 per linear foot of cabinetry. A 60″ double-sink vanity is about 5 linear feet - from roughly $600 - and your free 3D design prices the exact width, finish and configuration before you order.

Will the cabinets handle bathroom humidity?

Yes - that is the point of the build. ½″ plywood boxes with moisture-tolerant finishes keep their shape and screw-holding strength in damp rooms, where particleboard swells. Drawer hardware is soft-close and rated for daily use.

Do you make floating vanities?

Yes. The wall-mounted double-sink floating vanity comes in 60″ and 72″ widths and mounts to studs with a reinforced cleat, like an upper cabinet. Floor-standing Shaker vanities are available from compact singles to full double runs.

Can I match my vanity to my kitchen?

Yes. Vanities are available in the same door styles and finishes as our kitchen lines - see all six door collections on the door styles page, and order free samples to confirm the match.
