Designed around how you store

A closet system earns its keep in the layout, not the boxes: double-hang rails for everyday clothes, long-hang for coats and dresses, angled shelves for shoes, drawers for everything that shouldn't be on display. Our closet systems are fully modular - send your wall lengths and ceiling height and we plan the elevation wall by wall, balancing hanging, shelving and drawer storage around your actual wardrobe rather than a one-size template. Shelving is adjustable after installation, so the system adapts as your storage changes.

Furniture-grade construction

Carcasses are ½″ cabinet-grade plywood with UV-coated interiors - no particleboard - the same build standard as our kitchens. Drawer banks run dovetailed solid-wood boxes on soft-close undermount glides. Finishes run from warm oak woodgrains to deep walnut; free samples ship before you commit. Everything is certified to TSCA Title VI, KCMA and CARB2 standards.

How ordering works

Send room dimensions and a photo, and we return a free 3D design with an itemized quote priced per linear foot. The system ships flat, ready to assemble, from six U.S. warehouses, and goes together with standard tools - a weekend project for a handy homeowner, or a quick install for your contractor.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a walk-in closet system cost?

Closet systems start at $149.99 per linear foot of cabinetry. An 8-foot closet wall starts around $1,200; a full walk-in with three fitted walls is quoted exactly from your free 3D design, which balances hanging, shelving and drawers to your wardrobe.

Are the systems custom-sized?

Yes - every system is modular and configured to your room. Send wall lengths and ceiling height and we plan the layout; custom dimensions and finishes are available on request.

How hard is assembly?

The system ships flat with hardware included and assembles with standard tools. Most homeowners treat a single-wall system as a weekend project; contractors install full walk-ins in a day or two.

What finishes are available?

Warm oak and deep walnut woodgrains are the signature closet finishes, and the systems are also available in the painted finishes from our door collections. Free samples ship before you order.
